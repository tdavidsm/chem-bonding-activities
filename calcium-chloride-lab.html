This is a practice run of the real lab. The simulation is simplified: the atom views are flat (2-D), electrons are drawn as dots, and the time-lapse is much faster than real life. Your real data will be a little different.

Practice the lab before you do it

Walk through the same steps you will do in class, in the same order. Everything on the bench is touch-friendly: drag equipment with a finger or mouse, and tap buttons and items.

Bench viewHandle the equipment: balance, forceps, graduated cylinder, eyedropper, pH paper, ring stand and Bunsen burner.
🔬 Atom viewWhen a reaction happens, tap Zoom in to see what the atoms and electrons are doing.
AnalysisTurn your masses into pie charts and see which formula matches. You can type in your own real lab data there.

Electron colors: ● gold = electrons that came from calcium   ● blue = a water molecule's own electrons   ● violet = electrons from the hydrogen of HCl
