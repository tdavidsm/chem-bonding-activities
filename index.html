What's happening

Models are simplified on purpose: atoms are drawn as Bohr models and everything is flat (2-D). Real atoms and crystals are 3-D and electrons are clouds, not dots.

Three ways atoms stick together

Work through all six activities (click the tabs). Each one earns a ⭐, and finishing all six gives you a completion code.

IonicMetal atoms give valence electrons to nonmetal atoms. Opposite charges attract and pack into a crystal lattice.
MetallicMetal atoms release valence electrons into a shared “sea” that flows around a lattice of positive cores.
CovalentNonmetal atoms share pairs of electrons. The shared electrons spend their time between the two nuclei.
